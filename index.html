Who I Am

About Me

I'm a second-year computer science student at the University of the Witwatersrand, majoring in computer science, computer applications and organisational psychology. The combination is deliberate: most systems fail at the point where they meet the people meant to use them, and I want to be useful on both sides of that line.

Python is my strongest language. On a group restaurant order tracking app I owned the MySQL database layer and built user authentication in PHP, and I've built a business intelligence dashboard on AWS using QuickSight and SPICE. My certifications run through SAP business AI and autonomous enterprise concepts, Google project management, a Wharton business foundations specialisation, and the AWS AI & ML Scholars programme, where I'm currently working through an agentic AI nanodegree.

I've tutored with Teach Me 2 since September 2022, teaching mathematics, science subjects and English to learners from intermediate phase through to matric. I'm based in Johannesburg and open to freelancing, vacation work and internships in software engineering, cloud and data. During term I work fully remotely, since I'm studying full time. I can take on in-office roles during the December to January holidays.
